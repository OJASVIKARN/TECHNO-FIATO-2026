Category 01 / Target Locked
Techélon

Screens, software, and split-second decisions — eight events testing everything from design instinct to raw code speed.

TE—01Editra

Participants will create a 30–60 second video based on a theme revealed on the day of the event, using the provided resources with effective editing, visuals, transitions, text, and sound design.

TE—02Morphotek

Participants will be given a UI/UX design theme or problem statement on the spot. They will have 1 hour to conceptualize, design, and present a functional interface based on the given theme.

TE—03Modelus Virtuo

Participants will receive a reference image and must recreate it in 3D using only the provided software. The challenge lies in precision, attention to detail, and effective use of 3D modeling tools.

TE—04Pixelis

Participants will be given a topic on the spot and must create a creative visual composition based on it using Adobe Photoshop, testing creativity, visual storytelling, and composition.

TE—05Bytegenesis

Participants will be given topics one week prior and must create an animated story on Scratch, communicating the chosen topic through storytelling, characters, dialogue, and movement.

TE—06Bytecoder

Participants will be given three programming problems to solve within the allotted time. The goal is efficient, well-structured code that produces the required output under pressure.

TE—07Arcbyte

A high-energy gaming showdown featuring Mortal Kombat and EA FC, where players battle head-to-head showcasing combat skills, strategy, reflexes, and competitive spirit.

TE—08Tech Nova

Teams present a fully functional software or hardware-based solution to a real-world problem, reflecting the innovation, practicality, and reliability of a market-ready product.

Category 02 / Target Locked
Machina Fiato

Where mechanical design meets strategy — six builds, six arenas, one shot to prove your machine outperforms the rest.

RB—01Robo Rash

An adrenaline-charged obstacle course that pushes robots to their limits — bots navigate ramps, cones, and boundaries in a race against the clock where precision matters as much as speed.

RB—02Maneuver

A test of robotic skill, precision, and speed — custom-built bots navigate a challenging track while completing object-placement tasks and checkpoints for the highest score.

RB—03Robo Rumble

An action-packed balloon battle where strategy meets chaos — two bots enter the arena armed with balloons, and the goal is to protect your own while popping your opponent's.

RB—04Hover Surge

A clash of engineering skill and control — custom-built hovercrafts face off head-to-head, each testing stability and maneuverability while trying to push the opponent off the platform.

RB—05GOB

A clash of engineering skill and durability — custom-built machines battle it out in a test of strength, control, and resilience, staying in the arena until the last second.

RB—06Ballista

A high-energy robotics challenge inspired by robotic football — teams design and control bots to outscore opponents, where accuracy, control, and teamwork decide the champions.
